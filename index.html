Methodology

How I Approach Every Engagement

The same four-step discipline applies whether I'm analyzing delivery SLAs or architecting a RevOps pipeline from scratch.

01

Define the Business Problem

Before any data is pulled, I define the exact business question and what a decision-ready answer looks like. Scope prevents scope creep.

02

Collect & Structure the Data

Pull from the right sources, resolve quality issues, and model the data correctly. Garbage in, garbage out — this step is non-negotiable.

03

Analyze & Surface the Signal

Use SQL, Python, or Excel to segment, compare, and find the patterns that explain the problem — not just describe it.

04

Recommend & Measure Impact

Translate insight into operational action with clear ownership, timeline, and a trackable metric. If it can't be measured, it didn't happen.
